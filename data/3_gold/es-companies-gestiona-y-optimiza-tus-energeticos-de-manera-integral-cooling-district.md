Distrito térmico
At Celsia, we revolutionize energy solutions so that urban and business projects can evolve. We combine technologies that interact with each other to optimize the production and distribution of thermal energy for cooling, heating, hot water and electric power systems.
With this solution you can reduce up to 50 % of your initial investment in electrical infrastructure and air conditioning for your project.
