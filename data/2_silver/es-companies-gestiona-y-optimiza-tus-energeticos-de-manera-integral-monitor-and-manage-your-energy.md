Soluciones en eficiencia energética para empresas y constructoras
Gestiona y optimiza tus energéticos de manera integral
Monitoreo y gestión de energéticos
At Celsia, we believe in the value of data to make your production process more efficient by identifying opportunities for improvement. Thanks to the constant monitoring and follow-up of energy efficiency indicators, you will achieve savings that enhance competitiveness and contribute to the environment.
How does it work?
We use the Internet of industrial things to monitor energy consumption (submetering) and other critical variables of your process.
With our monitoring system, you will have a custom-designed information dashboard to track your process energy variables in real time.
In addition, through data science techniques we identify opportunities to improve your processes.
Monitoring, the first step in digitalization!
It implements alternatives (improvements) without an initial investment.
It measures electrical variables at the circuit level.
It identifies opportunities for savings with a team of experts.
Get up-to-date, reliable information.
It identifies the most energy-intensive processes and make efficient decisions.
