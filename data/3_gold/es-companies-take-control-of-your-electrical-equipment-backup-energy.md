The future is today! We take your company to the next level
Thinking about you and your company’s needs, we designed three business models for you:
Plan Luz
With this model, you avoid making unnecessary investments in assets that do not generate income, you only pay a fixed monthly fee for the service and, that’s it, you have a reliable backup system and 24/7 emergency attention and maintenance.
Direct sales
We guide you and offer you a backup system that fits your needs, assisting you from the design phase to the execution of the project. To help you revolutionize and evolve your business, we can finance the purchase of your assets.
Maintenance
Thinking about the constant evolution of your company, we developed maintenance service programs for power plants performed by specialized personnel, trained and certified by Cummins Power Generation.
