# Improving school infrastructure

Inicio
About us
Foundation
Improving school infrastructure
Through our Enciende program
We minimize health risks to the educational community due to lack of water and basic sanitation, or due to electrical hazards.
We create conditions to stay in school.
We support teachers’ dedication to their work of educating, preventing them from investing time in solving infrastructure problems.
We dignify spaces and make them safe and healthy.
We inform the educational community on how to use water and energy responsibly and efficiently.
