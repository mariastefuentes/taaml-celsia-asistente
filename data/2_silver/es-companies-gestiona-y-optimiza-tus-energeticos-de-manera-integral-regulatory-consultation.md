Asesoría regulatoria
The transformation of the electricity market implies constant changes in regulatory issues and at Celsia, knowledge is a tool available to our customers. So, we help you to know and understand the new regulatory policies and how they directly or indirectly impact your business.
How do we do it?
Our regulatory team is at your disposal to conduct a review and training on market policies and, together with our technical team, find the best solution for your company.
We review reactive penalties, clauses in energy purchase contracts, voltage level, unregulated market policies, carbon tax, and others.
