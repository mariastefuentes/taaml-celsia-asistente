Respetamos los derechos humanos
El respeto y la promoción de los derechos humanos son pilares
fundamentales de todo lo que hacemos. En Celsia, cada decisión y acción
está guiada por este compromiso, y nos aseguramos de que estos principios
se cumplan en nuestras operaciones y en las relaciones con nuestros grupos de interés.
Estamos comprometidos con el respeto de los derechos humanos y lo priorizamos en todas nuestras operaciones y relaciones comerciales. Esto orienta nuestras acciones hacia los grupos de interés, la cadena de valor, la forma en que logramos resultados y la sostenibilidad de la compañía. Promovemos, respetamos y valoramos:.
• El trabajo libre y voluntario.
• La salud, la protección y la seguridad en la empresa..
• La educación y el desarrollo de niños, niñas y jóvenes.
• La libertad, la igualdad y la no discriminación.
• La libertad de asociación y la negociación colectiva.
• El derecho a la igualdad en el trabajo.
• Decent working conditions.
• Las condiciones dignas de trabajo.
• La vida familiar.
• El derecho a la privacidad.
• Los derechos de todas las comunidades étnicas.
• El derecho a un ambiente sano.
Proceso de debida diligencia
Nuestras decisiones y las acciones que emprendemos están enmarcadas en el respeto y la promoción de los derechos humanos. Como parte de este compromiso, realizamos periódicamente un proceso de debida diligencia para asegurarnos de que se mantenga vigente y refleje el contexto de Celsia en cada una de las geografías donde operamos.
Hitos del proceso
We identify, evaluate, prevent, monitor and report the risks of human rights (HR) violations the company may face. This process is carried out through the Comprehensive Risk Management System (CRMS), which allows us to establish action plans when there is a moderate, high or critical risk. We have a Human Rights (HR) policy approved by senior management , which establishes the company’s commitment to prevent, respect and promote human rights in all operations. This includes requirements for our own operations, suppliers and business partners, as well as specific actions to comply with these requirements.Click here to learn more about the Human Rights Policy.
To learn about the Human Rights Policy
We include human rights clauses in contracts and agreements with suppliers and business partners. These set out their human rights commitments. In addition, we assess their compliance in operations, the value chain and joint ventures.
We develop HR for employees, contractors and prioritized suppliers. This training focuses on the United Nations Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights, due diligence, and business commitment and responsibility. The objective is to provide them with the knowledge and tools necessary to manage human rights in their work.
We constantly communicate with internal and external stakeholders regarding management in Human Rights (HR) matters. We do so through a variety of media, such as newsletters, the Integrated Report and the website.
100% of our operations were assessed for human rights in 2025.
100% of our joint ventures** were evaluated.
45.33% of our tier 1 contractors and suppliers* were evaluated over the past 3 years, with risks identified and mitigation remediation processes implemented.
*Tier 1 contractors and suppliers. For those with an active business relationship with us in the past three years, we conducted an evaluation of their performance in OHS, labor compliance, and human rights.
**Joint ventures. Through due diligence processes, we verify that partners have policies and controls that support their commitment to respect for human rights.
We have the transparency hotline that receives confidential information about any alleged breach of the Code of Business Conduct, as well as business ethics violations, including possible reports related to Human Rights

People Are at the Heart
of Our Actions
We recognize that people are the central axis of all our strategies. For this reason, we prevent and mitigate all negative impacts that our operations may have on Human Rights and the environment.
We treat our employees with respect and dignity through a safe, healthy work environment that is free of discrimination. We support transparency in this relationship and promote inclusion and diversity, ensuring the right to equal opportunities, non-discrimination and freedom of thought, culture, religion and political ideology.
We develop strategies to relate to communities and guarantee the mechanisms and spaces for their quick and adequate information, participation and consultation with respect to matters arising from our operations and the development of new projects that could affect them positively or negatively.
We support our suppliers to contribute to their grow th and contribute to the development of our areas of influence by hiring local suppliers.
The health and safety of our employees and contractors is our priority. We work to strengthen awareness of self-care and avoid accidents with the program: “I Choose to Take Care of Myself.”
